\subsection{Monotone CHT}
C++17. Insert lines $y=mx+b$ in the slope order selected by
\texttt{INCREASING\_SLOPES}; equal slopes are allowed.
\texttt{MIN\_QUERY} selects minimum (\texttt{true}) or maximum
(\texttt{false}). Defaults: minimum, nonincreasing slopes.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lll}
\textbf{Query} & \textbf{Slope order} & \textbf{\texttt{query(x)} order} \\
\hline
Minimum & Nonincreasing & Nondecreasing $x$ \\
Minimum & Nondecreasing & Nonincreasing $x$ \\
Maximum & Nonincreasing & Nonincreasing $x$ \\
Maximum & Nondecreasing & Nondecreasing $x$ \\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\texttt{add} and \texttt{query}: amortized $O(1)$;
\texttt{query\_bs}: $O(\log n)$ with arbitrary $x$ order,
without changing the query pointer. Insertions and queries may be
interleaved. Query only when nonempty. Inputs are signed 64-bit;
results are \texttt{\_\_int128\_t}.
\cppfile{../dp/CHT.cpp}


\subsection{CHT with Counts}
C++17. Returns \texttt{(value, cnt)}: optimize the value and prefer
smaller counts on ties, for both minimum and maximum queries.
Uses the slope and query orders in the Monotone CHT table above;
equal slopes are allowed. \texttt{add} and \texttt{query} take
amortized $O(1)$. Query only when nonempty.

This version compares \texttt{long double} values directly; the
count breaks ties only when computed values compare equal. Each
line keeps the supplied count. Both variants define \texttt{CHT};
use one definition at a time.
\cppfile{../dp/CHTCount.cpp}
